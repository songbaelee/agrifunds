Agricultural SME finance · Guatemala · 2025

8 CSAF members disbursed $34M to 19 agricultural businesses in Guatemala in 2025. Source: CSAF Open Data Portal

Which 8 lenders? Which 19 businesses? The data can't say.

CSAF members report their lending under a nondisclosure agreement, and CSAF publishes it only as an aggregate that identifies neither the lender nor the borrower. That's a deliberate trade-off: lenders share more data because it's anonymized. The result is that one of the best datasets on agri-SME finance can tell you how much went to Guatemala, but not who lent it or who received it.

This page reconstructs what the public record does show, using only what lenders publish themselves (partner pages, annual and quarterly reports) plus CSAF's own State of the Sector report. None of these relationships can be confirmed as part of CSAF's 2025 figure.
